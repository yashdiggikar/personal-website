Research & Projects

Featured — Ongoing

Prithvi-Dust

Adapting the NASA–IBM Prithvi WxC foundation model for global dust forecasting. Retains the pretrained backbone while adding dust-specific input/output mappings for five size-resolved tracers — surface and column dust mass, aerosol optical properties, emissions, sedimentation, and wet/dry deposition — trained on post-2000 MERRA-2 residuals. Uses attention- and embedding-based attribution to rank meteorological and process controls (winds, PBL height, precipitation, particle size, deposition) on regional and vertical dust bias, and benchmarks against Aurora on events including the 13 June 2022 Iraq sandstorm.
